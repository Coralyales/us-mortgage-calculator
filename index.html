Understanding Your US Mortgage Calculation

When purchasing real estate in the United States, understanding your total monthly financial commitment is crucial for maintaining long-term financial health. A standard US mortgage payment consists of four primary components often referred to as PITI: Principal, Interest, Taxes, and Insurance.

1. Principal and Interest (P&I)

The principal is the dollar amount that goes toward paying off the original loan balance, while interest is the cost paid to the lender for borrowing the money. The standard formula used to compute fixed-rate mortgage payments is:

M = P * [ r(1 + r)^n ] / [ (1 + r)^n - 1 ]

2. Property Taxes and Insurance

Local government municipalities collect annual property taxes to fund public services. Additionally, mortgage lenders require homeowners insurance to protect the structure against damage.
