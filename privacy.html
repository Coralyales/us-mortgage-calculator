Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

At US Mortgage Calculator ("we", "us", or "our"), accessible from https://usmortgagecalculator.com, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by us and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

Our website functions entirely as a client-side calculator. We do not store, log, or transmit any mortgage calculation inputs (such as home price, down payment, income, or interest rates) to external servers. All processing occurs locally within your web browser.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, US Mortgage Calculator uses "cookies". These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited.

3. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie & AdSense

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet.

4. Rights Under GDPR & CCPA Compliance

Under CCPA and GDPR, users have the right to request disclosure of personal data collected, request deletion of data, and request non-sale of personal data.

5. Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, contact us at support@usmortgagecalculator.com or via our Contact Page.
